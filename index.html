The Social Sports Games

A mix of games. A lot of surprises.

Beach relays, trust games, water games, speed runs: a rotating mix you'll never see twice. Every game is built to make you connect, compete, and laugh with your team. (And a little with the rival teams too. No promises who you'll text first on Sunday.)

✦Sample challenges. The actual line-up stays a surprise.

  • Guide your teammate through the course using only communication.

  • Move water down the line with speed, rhythm, and teamwork.

  • Pass, catch, rotate, and keep the sequence alive.

  • Move as one team through a sandy course. Coordination beats speed.

  • Strength matters, but timing and coordination win.

  • Launch, catch, and return without bursting the balloon.

  • A fast team relay where timing and transitions decide everything.

  • Pass the cup down the line without dropping or popping.

What to bring

  • 💧A big bottle of water. Non-negotiable. Barcelona in July is brutal sun, you'll thank yourself by 17:00.
  • 🧴Sunscreen. Re-apply between games. The sand reflects everything back at you.
  • 🩳Swimsuit + a top you can move and get wet in.
  • 😎Your social + sports skills. We bring the rest.

How it works

Teams. Games. Medals. Tapas & DJ.

Every edition follows the same arc: arrive at 16:30, final whistle at 19:00, then tapas and a DJ at the beach bar until late.

  1. 16:30

    Welcome tent

    Registration on Platja del Fòrum, a mini icebreaker game, then your team colour. Bandana on, meet your crew, cook up a chant.

  2. 16:45

    Opening ceremony

    Format walkthrough. Icebreaker games: memory, strategy and teamwork. Loud, fast, no overthinking.

  3. 17:00 – 19:00

    The surprise games

    New categories, new format every edition. Live scoreboard, watch your team climb. A rotating selection of beach games designed to cut through language barriers and get you moving. One big finale.

  4. 19:00 – 20:00

    Medals + tapas + DJ

    Awards ceremony, stupid awards, group photo on the sand. Then a loaded tapas spread on us, sunset, a DJ, and an optional afterparty at Sun Sea Bar.

How it works

Teams. Games. Medals. Tapas & DJ.

Every edition follows the same arc: arrive at 16:30, final whistle at 19:00, then tapas and a DJ at the beach bar until late.

  1. 16:30

    Welcome tent

    Registration, a quick icebreaker, then your team colour. Bandana on, meet your crew.

  2. 16:45

    Opening ceremony

    Format walkthrough. Icebreaker games: memory, strategy, teamwork. Loud, fast, no overthinking.

  3. 17:00 – 19:00

    The surprise games

    New games every edition. Live scoreboard, one big finale.

  4. 19:00 – 20:00

    Medals + tapas + DJ

    Awards, group photo, then tapas, sunset, a DJ. Optional afterparty at Sun Sea Bar.

The games

40+ games. Nobody sits out.

From icebreakers that get strangers high-fiving (or hugging) in the first five minutes, to a Minefield finale that gets everyone laughing and creating unique memories. The line-up changes every edition.

🪢

Tug of War

The natural closer. Everyone plays, everyone shouts, everyone ends up in the sand.

10–40 players · ~10 min

🌊

Sea Relay

Swim out, round the buoy, run back and tag. The most requested addition from the Edition II survey.

8–24 players · ~20 min

🎈

Water Balloon Toss

Pairs toss and step back. Cooling, hilarious and everyone can play.

8–40 players · ~15 min

💣

Minefield

Three blindfolded runners are guided by two callers through an obstacle course while collecting objects against the clock.

5+ per group · ~15 min

🦁

Lions & Gazelles

A reaction-sprint chase in pairs. The whole group explodes into a race at once — instant adrenaline, loud crowd.

6–30 players · ~10 min

🛍️

Sack Race

Retro, ridiculous, extremely photogenic.

8–32 players · ~10 min

What to bring

  • 💧A big bottle of water. Non-negotiable. Barcelona in July is brutal sun, you'll thank yourself by 17:00.
  • 🧴Sunscreen. Re-apply between games. The sand reflects everything back at you.
  • 🩳Swimsuit + a top you can move and get wet in.
  • 😎Your social + sports skills. We bring the rest.

What you're playing for

A real trophy. With its own tech.

Every edition crowns one team: a physical trophy with an NFC chip in the base, plus a medal for every player.

  • A trophy that's actually yours

  • Tap to unlock

    Built into the base. Tap any phone and the trophy opens its own page.

  • The story, not just the score

    Recap video, winning roster, final score — all tied to that one trophy.

In short

What is an Olympic-style company retreat?

It is a multi-day company retreat where mixed teams compete in relay, trust and coordination games for points on a live scoreboard, with an opening ceremony, medals and a closing ceremony that recognises everyone. Barcelona Games runs it end to end: two co-hosts lead the programme, a small assistant team runs the details, and the itinerary is planned from arrival to send-off.

Questions

What HR teams ask us.

Can you run an Olympic-style company retreat?

Yes. It is what we do. Teams get a colour and a bandana, compete in relay, trust and coordination games for points on a live scoreboard, and finish with a closing ceremony with medals, speeches and recognition for everyone. It is built to include everyone, not only the sporty ones.

Who leads the retreat?

Two co-hosts lead the whole programme: Quentin Patrouillard, the founder, and a co-host leader. They are supported by a small assistant team for games, scoring, logistics and safety. No agency hand-off: the same people plan it and run it on site.

Is there a full itinerary, or do we have to plan it?

A full itinerary comes with the retreat. Every hour from arrival to departure is planned: opening ceremony, meals, games, optional hike, breathwork, closing ceremony. The page above shows the real itinerary of our first retreat and a sample for companies. We customise it on a planning call.

How many people and how many nights?

10 to 40 people and 2 to 4 nights. The standard shape is two nights, Friday to Sunday. Our current partner villa sleeps 28, and a second residence nearby takes the group up to 40.

Where does it take place?

In a private villa in Catalonia, about 2 hours from Barcelona, in the Pyrenees. The location can change with budget, group size or preference, and we can organise it anywhere internationally on request.

What does it cost?

Corporate retreats are priced from about €10,000 to €20,000 depending on group size, number of nights and what you add. Weekends are booked about 2 months ahead, weekdays about 1 month. Smaller one-session team building starts at €700.

What is included?

The villa, a private chef and the meals, the full programme and the 80+ game library, the co-hosts and assistant team, professional breathwork and meditation facilitators, and a photo and video team with a highlight trailer. Transport, workshops and an ice bath are optional extras.

What if it rains?

Every day has an indoor back-up. At our first retreat the outdoor Olympics were followed by an indoor finale with pool and table tennis planned for rain. Hikes depend on weather and timings adapt.

What did the first retreat look like?

Our first Weekend Games retreat ran 16 to 18 October 2026 in the Pyrenees: opening ceremony, a waterfall hike, outdoor Olympics, an all-white dinner, breathwork and a closing ceremony, plus extra surprises such as an outdoor jacuzzi, a secret hunt and games developed in secret during the weekend. That one was a social retreat for people meeting each other. A company retreat uses the same format built around your team.

Friday

🤝 Connect

Settle in. Meet the team. Non-stop from arrival to lights out.

  • Arrive & settle in. Check in, find your room, welcome drink on the terrace.
  • Bandana on, meet your crew. Team assignment doubles as your first icebreaker.
  • Icebreaker games, back to back. A few quick rounds and every name is learned, every guard is down.
  • Dinner, then evening games. One long table, shared plates, then lighter games before the night winds down.

Nothing after dinner is mandatory — an early night is a completely normal choice.

Saturday

🏆 Play

Team colours on. 12+ games and challenges across the day, all building one scoreboard.

  • Morning — hike or sleep in. An optional sunrise hike for early risers. Breakfast either way.
  • Warm-up games. A few quick rounds to get everyone moving before the real competition starts.
  • The Mini Olympics. Tailored team challenges, relays and problem-solving, back to back, building toward the finale.
  • Awards, dinner, more games. Medal ceremony, a big feast, then evening games and room to enjoy the night.

Optional add-on: ice bath recovery, arranged as an extra.

Sunday

🧘 Reset

The one day built for slowing down. Even here, nothing is mandatory.

  • A slower start. Breakfast together, yoga included if your team wants it.
  • Guided breathwork and meditation. A coached session to switch off. Participation is optional.
  • Lunch and a proper send-off. Share the highlights, close together, depart in the afternoon.

The games

40+ games. Nobody sits out.

From icebreakers that get strangers high-fiving (or hugging) in the first five minutes, to a Minefield finale that gets everyone laughing and creating unique memories. The line-up changes every edition.

🪢

Tug of War

The natural closer. Everyone plays, everyone shouts, everyone ends up in the sand.

10–40 players · ~10 min

🌊

Sea Relay

Swim out, round the buoy, run back and tag. The most requested addition from the Edition II survey.

8–24 players · ~20 min

🎈

Water Balloon Toss

Pairs toss and step back. Cooling, hilarious and everyone can play.

8–40 players · ~15 min

💣

Minefield

Three blindfolded runners are guided by two callers through an obstacle course while collecting objects against the clock.

5+ per group · ~15 min

🦁

Lions & Gazelles

A reaction-sprint chase in pairs. The whole group explodes into a race at once — instant adrenaline, loud crowd.

6–30 players · ~10 min

🛍️

Sack Race

Retro, ridiculous, extremely photogenic.

8–32 players · ~10 min

What to bring

  • 💧A big bottle of water. Non-negotiable. Barcelona in July is brutal sun, you'll thank yourself by 17:00.
  • 🧴Sunscreen. Re-apply between games. The sand reflects everything back at you.
  • 🩳Swimsuit + a top you can move and get wet in.
  • 😎Your social + sports skills. We bring the rest.

There's nothing else quite like this weekend in Barcelona. We priced this first edition to prove the value above, not to profit from it.

Three days. Zero planning.

We take care of the villa, meals, and full programme, from Mini Olympics and a natural cold plunge to a special hike, breathwork, meditation, and workshops designed to leave you with more energy, better connections, and a few new ideas.

Who this is for.

Mostly mid-20s to mid-30s internationals in Barcelona who love games, want to meet new people, are up for trying something different, and enjoy hanging out with other curious minds.

Think founders, freelancers, people in tech, creatives, consultants, remote workers, and generally people who want to meet others beyond the usual work / drinks / run-club loop.

You're coming to play hard, switch off, learn something new, reset, and meet genuinely interesting people, with new friendships, fresh ideas, and a lot of ridiculous games along the way.

Where you'll be staying

A private villa in the mountains, exclusively ours for the weekend.

This isn't a public retreat centre, it's a private villa booked out exclusively for our group of 20: massive grounds, big indoor halls to chill and run workshops in, wide open space for the mini-Olympics, a hiking trail right from the door, and natural waterfalls and swimming holes a short walk away. No one else will be there.

Friday

🤝 Connect

Arrive solo or with friends at a private villa in the mountains outside Barcelona: 20 people, mostly mid-20s to mid-30s.

Connection and icebreaker games kick off the evening so the whole group actually meets. We might ask you to prepare something small ahead of time to help with the introductions.

Saturday

🏆 Play

Early risers can join an optional hike (or surprise activity) in the morning, led by Quentin, before a full day of mini-Olympics and team challenges in the afternoon.

Same competitive spirit as our beach editions, with a shared scoreboard running the whole weekend.

We'll assign teams ahead of time and ask you to bring a plain t-shirt in your team's colour. Paired with the bandana you'll get on-site, it makes spotting your crew out on the field a lot easier.

The day closes with a medal ceremony, trophies for the winning team, then a party with more games and a big feast to refuel on protein. The estate's natural cold pool is right there for anyone who wants to recover like a champion.

Sunday

🧘 Reset

Slow down. Soak it in.

We open with The Journey Home, an immersive meditation and breathwork experience to pause, reconnect, and reset. Then it's time to play, enjoy the villa, and hang out with your new crew. A gentle meditation brings us together again before or after lunch, we'll follow the group's rhythm.

As the day winds down, there's room for one last adventure: a hike, a waterfall visit, or exploring nearby activities before hugs and goodbyes.

Round-trip transport is included in your ticket. Quentin arranges carpools (around five cars) so the whole group travels to the villa together, at no extra cost.

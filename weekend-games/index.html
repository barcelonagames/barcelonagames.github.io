Where you'll be staying

A private villa in the Pyrenees, exclusively ours.

Friday

🤝 Connect

Arrive solo or with friends. Settle in, find your room, meet the group.

Icebreaker games kick off the evening so everyone actually meets: easy, funny, no experience needed, nobody left standing on the side. Then a shared dinner.

Saturday

🏆 Play

An optional morning hike led by Quentin, then a full day of Mini Olympics and team challenges.

Same competitive spirit as our beach editions, with teams, bandanas and one scoreboard running the whole weekend. Bring a plain t-shirt in your team colour.

The day closes with a medal ceremony, trophies, a big feast and more games. The villa's natural cold pool is right there for recovery.

Sunday

🧘 Reset

Slow down. Soak it in.

We open with The Journey Home, an immersive meditation and breathwork experience to pause, reconnect and reset. Then time to play, enjoy the villa and hang out with your new crew.

As the day winds down, one last adventure (a hike or a waterfall visit) before hugs and goodbyes.

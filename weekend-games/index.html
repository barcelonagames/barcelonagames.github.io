Friday

Connect

Arrive solo or with friends at a countryside villa outside Barcelona — 20 people for now, ages 25 to 40, here to actually meet each other.

Connection and icebreaker games kick off the evening so the whole group actually meets — we might ask you to prepare something small ahead of time to help with the introductions.

Saturday

Play

A full day of mini-Olympics and team challenges, same competitive spirit as our beach editions — with a shared scoreboard running the whole weekend.

Sunday

Reset

The morning slows down. A guest facilitator joins for the send-off.

Dan Green

Immersive Inner Journeys. Breathwork, music, philosophy, place.

✅ All included: kick-ass villa, chef-cooked meals, Mini Olympics, ice bath, hike, breathwork, workshops & 20 great people. You just show up.

Three days. Zero planning.

We take care of the villa, meals, and full programme, from Mini Olympics and an ice bath to a special hike, breathwork, meditation, and workshops designed to leave you with more energy, better connections, and a few new ideas.

Who this is for

25–35ish internationals living in Barcelona who are curious, active, and building interesting lives here.

Think founders, freelancers, people in tech, creatives, consultants, remote workers, and generally people who want to meet others beyond the usual work / drinks / run-club loop.

You're coming to play hard, switch off, learn something new, reset, and meet genuinely interesting people, with new friendships, fresh ideas, and a lot of ridiculous games along the way.

Where you'll be staying

Finca Sanillés, a private estate in the Cadí mountains.

The whole place is ours for the weekend: massive grounds, big indoor halls to chill and run workshops in, wide open space for the mini-Olympics, a hiking trail right from the door, and natural waterfalls and swimming holes a short walk away.

Friday

🤝 Connect

Arrive solo or with friends at Finca Sanillés, a private estate outside Barcelona: 20 people, ages 25 to 35.

Connection and icebreaker games kick off the evening so the whole group actually meets. We might ask you to prepare something small ahead of time to help with the introductions.

Saturday

🏆 Play

Early risers can join an optional hike (or surprise activity) in the morning, led by Quentin, before a full day of mini-Olympics and team challenges in the afternoon.

Same competitive spirit as our beach editions, with a shared scoreboard running the whole weekend.

The day closes with a medal ceremony, trophies for the winning team, then a party with more games and a big feast to refuel on protein. An ice bath will be set up for anyone who wants to recover like a champion.

Sunday

🧘 Reset

The morning slows down. A guest facilitator joins for the send-off.

Dan Green

Immersive Inner Journeys. Breathwork, music, philosophy, place.

Follow @dan.byregal ↗

Sunday opens with two guided breathwork journeys led by Dan Green. Music, breath, and a room full of people who were strangers on Friday. Inspired by the idea that what's meant for you won't pass you by.

Good to know

🚗 Getting there

Quentin organizes carpools between attendees. Some already have a car heading out, others need a seat. A small separate cost, split between riders.

🍽️ Food

A home-cooked Spanish menu from a local cook: Cerdanya specialties, with a vegetarian option. Real sports-weekend fuel, not catering-hall food.

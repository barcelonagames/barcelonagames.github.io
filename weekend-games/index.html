Three days in the Pyrenees. Zero planning.

We handle the villa, the meals and the full programme: Mini Olympics, a natural cold plunge, a mountain hike, breathwork, meditation and workshops. You leave with more energy, better friendships and a few new ideas.

Who this retreat is for.

Mostly mid-20s to mid-30s internationals in Barcelona who want a real reset without going full monk. You like to move, you like to compete, and you're curious about people.

Founders, freelancers, tech, creatives, consultants and remote workers who want something beyond the usual work / drinks / run-club loop.

Where you'll be staying

A private villa in the Pyrenees, exclusively ours.

A whole villa booked out for our group alone: big grounds, indoor halls for workshops and chilling, open space for the Mini Olympics, a hiking trail from the door, and waterfalls and natural swimming holes a short walk away.

Friday

🤝 Connect

Arrive solo or with friends. Settle in, find your room, meet the group.

Icebreaker games kick off the evening so everyone actually meets: easy, funny, no experience needed, nobody left standing on the side. Then a shared dinner.

🍸 Friday night

Games first. Moscow Mule after.

Once the icebreaker games wind down, drinks from our sponsor Batovi come out (a Moscow Mule made in Barcelona, optional) and the group keeps talking. Not drinking? NOCCO and El Tony Maté have you covered.

Meet Batovi ↗18+ only. Enjoy responsibly.

Saturday

🏆 Play

An optional morning hike led by Quentin, then a full day of Mini Olympics and team challenges.

Same competitive spirit as our beach editions, with teams, bandanas and one scoreboard running the whole weekend. Bring a plain t-shirt in your team colour.

The day closes with a medal ceremony, trophies, a big feast and more games. The villa's natural cold pool is right there for recovery.

Sunday

🧘 Reset

Slow down. Soak it in.

We open with The Journey Home, an immersive meditation and breathwork experience to pause, reconnect and reset. Then time to play, enjoy the villa and hang out with your new crew.

As the day winds down, one last adventure (a hike or a waterfall visit) before hugs and goodbyes.

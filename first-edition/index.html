Before Edition 01

How this got started.

One Saturday in April 2026, we ran the concept as a private trial with 30 friends: five teams, four challenges, medals, a long lunch. The idea was simple: see if strangers could really become a crew in one afternoon.

It worked too well to keep private. Three months later, Edition 01 filled a whole beach. Now we're running it monthly on Platja del Fòrum.

The clip on the left is the original April recap, filmed on a teammate's phone the morning after. The full April behind-the-scenes is preserved as the club's origin story.

Edition 04 is next. Saturday, 12 September 2026.

Four editions filled up already. Only 2 left on the beach this season, then our partner bar closes for the year, so this really is it for a while.

The Road to the Games

Rules & prize

What this is

A free driving game before The Weekend Games (16–18 October 2026). Five people fill a car pool. Each of you drives it from Barcelona to the finca: 45 seconds, steer with your thumb, collect what the weekend is about (new friends, a mountain escape, the feast, the party, medals and the trophy), and dodge toros, the ruc català, cones, roadworks and tractors. Your bandana colours the car.

Playing does not reserve a place on the retreat. Each person books their own place on Luma, subject to availability. The car in the game is not your real transport; carpools for the retreat are arranged separately.

Scoring

  • Collect the weekend on the way: Feast 10 · Party 10 · New friends 20 · Mountain escape 20 · BG medal 25 · Team bandana 50 · BG trophy 75 points.
  • Grab things in a row without crashing for a combo: x2 after 6, x3 after 12. A crash resets it and slows you down.
  • You drive in sets of 2 runs, and a set's runs add up. A run counts as used when it starts. When a set is done, start a fresh one as often as you like: your best set is your score.
  • Your car's score = the five players' best sets added up. Every seat matters. Ties go to the car that finished first.
  • Scores are checked against the course the server generated for that run. Impossible scores are rejected.

Who can win

  • Five different real people, one player per phone, and all five have driven at least once.
  • At least one seat filled through matching by someone outside the host's own group (not on the same network as the people who created or were invited into the car).
  • No unresolved reports on the car.
  • Before any prize is given, we check the top car by contacting every member through the Instagram or email they gave. Duplicate or fake players disqualify the car.

The car discount

We match you with your fiercest competitors for the retreat. Together you unlock a discount: your car needs to be full (five real people, at least one matched stranger) and reach a combined score of 8000 points or more, which is close to a perfect drive for everyone.

The discount amount and terms will be published here before anything is applied.

Be decent

First names only. You can leave a car or report it at any time from the car screen; reported cars are hidden until a real person at Barcelona Games has looked at them. We store your first name, scores, and optional contact only to run the game and verify winners. Questions or deletion requests: socialsportsgames.barcelona@gmail.com.
